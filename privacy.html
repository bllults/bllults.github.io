Privacy Policy & Zero-Telemetry Standard

Pure Code operates on a strict local-first and zero-knowledge architecture. Your files, database schemas, and personal interactions remain on your device.

01. Scope & Identity

This Privacy Policy applies to all software applications, developer utilities, and web services published by Pure Code (registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under KVK: 98970631, located in Oss, the Netherlands).

This covers our consumer and developer client applications distributed via the Apple App Store, macOS binaries, and our enterprise web platforms, including but not limited to VaultMail / ArchiveMail Viewer, DopaFlow, and PureSchema.

02. Local-First File Processing (MBOX, Archives, Schemas)

For our file parsing and viewer utilities (such as ArchiveMail Viewer):

  • No Server Uploads: When you open email archives (MBOX, EML, PST) or data files, parsing is executed entirely in-memory on your local Apple device (iOS/macOS sandbox).
  • Zero Cloud Telemetry: Files, attachments, email headers, and message bodies never leave your physical hardware. We run no analytical servers and maintain zero access logs.
  • No Temporary Storage: When you close or terminate the application, memory buffers are completely reclaimed by the operating system.

03. Data We Do NOT Collect

WE NEVER COLLECT, STORE, OR TRANSMIT:
  • • Personally Identifiable Information (Names, addresses, personal phone numbers).
  • • Location Data or GPS coordinates.
  • • Device identifiers (IDFA, MAC address, advertising identifiers).
  • • Keystrokes, search queries inside local files, or productivity logs.

04. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

If our mobile applications offer paid features, tip jars, or subscriptions:

All payment processing and financial transactions are handled solely by Apple Inc. via StoreKit and the App Store. Pure Code does not receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking information. Apple handles all billing under their respective terms and privacy policies.

05. Enterprise Web Services & AI Workflows

For clients participating in the PureSchema private pilot or using web features connected to LLM APIs (e.g., Anthropic Claude models):

  • Data processed via enterprise API endpoints operates strictly under zero-retention policies.
  • Enterprise schema definitions and relational topologies are strictly ephemeral and are never utilized to train or fine-tune foundation AI models.

06. European GDPR Compliance & Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU 2016/679), European users retain statutory rights regarding their personal data:

Because our native client apps function locally and collect no telemetry or user profiles, we do not hold personal databases to access, rectify, or erase. However, if you contact us directly via email, your correspondence is maintained solely to answer technical support requests and will be purged upon written request.
